# Odin Scene Template

Copy this template for each shot. Keep the identity block unchanged; vary only the scene block.

## Identity block — locked

Odin, the same fictional male character in every image, apparent age 27, tall athletic functional build, warm olive-brown complexion, angular face, strong jaw, high cheekbones, straight nose, deep-set hazel-brown eyes, long shoulder-length wavy dark-brown weathered hair, dense dark beard with a defined moustache, natural skin pores and subtle sun exposure. Quietly intelligent assessing gaze, restrained confidence, economical movement. High-end global artifact hunter; premium layered technical clothing in weathered black, charcoal, sand, and bone. Preserve exact facial geometry, apparent age, hair length, beard silhouette, eye color, and body proportions from the approved identity references.

## Negative constraints

Different actor, face drift, age change, short hair, clean-shaven, eye-color change, plastic skin, bodybuilder proportions, fantasy armor, Viking costume, superhero suit, vacant model expression, extra fingers, fused hands, changing garment construction, changing accessories, floating objects, inconsistent shadows, speed ramping, frame interpolation artifacts, text, watermark.

## Motion rule

Specify one subject action and one camera action. Use an approved still as the first-frame reference. Do not ask the model to redesign wardrobe, change location, and perform complex motion in the same clip.
