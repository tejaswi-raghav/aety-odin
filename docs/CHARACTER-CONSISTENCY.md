# Odin Character Consistency

This document turns the character bible into repeatable production constraints. The JSON identity contract is authoritative for fixed facts; this guide explains how to apply it.

## Reference hierarchy

Use references in this order:

1. Approved neutral face sheet: front, three-quarter, profile, and expression range.
2. Approved full-body sheet: front, rear, and walking pose with fixed proportions.
3. Current campaign wardrobe references.
4. Location, lighting, and cinematography references.

Never let a style or location reference override identity references.

## What remains fixed

- Facial proportions, apparent age, complexion, eye color, hair line, hair length, and beard silhouette
- Height impression, shoulder-to-waist relationship, limb proportions, and athletic build
- Quiet, assessing performance and economical movement
- The high-end artifact-hunter wardrobe language

## What may change

- Dirt, rain, sweat, minor tracked injuries, and the way hair reacts to weather
- Garment layers and textiles when the silhouette and story remain coherent
- Lens, camera movement, time of day, and environment
- Emotional intensity within the defined performance range

## Reference-pack standard

Before a new campaign, approve at least 12 identity images:

- Four face angles in flat neutral light
- Three controlled expressions: neutral, half-smile, focused strain
- Front, rear, and side full-body views
- One walking pose and one seated pose

Use the same approved pack for the entire production. Replacing a reference halfway through a sequence requires a continuity review.

## Naming and records

Every approved generation should retain model/version, prompt, negative prompt, seed when available, source reference IDs, aspect ratio, generation date, and reviewer. Store campaign outputs under a stable shot ID such as `ODN_NAM_001_SH030_V04`.

## Failure conditions

Reject an output if a casual viewer could interpret it as a different actor, if the apparent age changes materially, if signature facial hair changes, if body proportions drift between adjacent shots, or if the design becomes fantasy costume instead of ÆTY ONE utility.
