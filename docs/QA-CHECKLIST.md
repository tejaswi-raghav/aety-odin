# Continuity QA Checklist

## Identity

- [ ] Face matches the approved front and three-quarter references
- [ ] Apparent age remains 27
- [ ] Hazel-brown eyes, hairline, hair length, wave pattern, and beard silhouette match
- [ ] Skin texture is natural and complexion is stable under the scene lighting
- [ ] Body height impression and proportions match adjacent shots

## Performance

- [ ] Gaze is intelligent and assessing
- [ ] Movement has weight, balance, and a clear motivation
- [ ] Pre-awakening Odin does not perform visible supernatural power
- [ ] Expression stays within the approved range

## Wardrobe and props

- [ ] Garments match the approved look front and back
- [ ] Closures, pockets, seams, logos, and layers do not mutate within a shot
- [ ] Watch wrist, pendant, camera, and pack remain spatially consistent
- [ ] No accidental fantasy armor or generic superhero styling

## Image and motion

- [ ] Hands, teeth, eyes, hair edges, and contact points survive frame-by-frame review
- [ ] Lighting direction and shadows remain coherent
- [ ] Camera motion and subject motion do not produce morphing
- [ ] No background people merge with Odin or his vehicle

## Release

- [ ] Prompt, model, version, references, date, and reviewer are logged
- [ ] Music, footage, fonts, products, and reference material have usage rights
- [ ] Fictional-AI disclosure is visible at the account or campaign level
- [ ] Final master and web derivative are archived
